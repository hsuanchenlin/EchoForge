# EchoForge 0.2.2

A fix for dictation that produced nothing at all, and an end to failures that said nothing.

## Dictation that silently produced nothing

If EchoForge recorded, showed "Transcribing…", and then simply gave you nothing - no text
pasted, no entry in the history, no error - this release fixes it.

The cause was the first-run screen. When a speech model was already on your Mac - after a
reinstall, or because another build had downloaded one - that model was preselected for
you, complete with its checkmark and an enabled **Continue** button. What was never saved
was the choice itself. The app came out of setup with no engine selected, and every
recording afterwards failed to load one, threw your audio away and wrote a single line to
a log nobody sees.

Three things changed:

- **The first-run screen now saves the model it shows as selected**, whether you picked it
  or it was picked for you. It will not let you past setup with a model it cannot load.
- **Existing installations repair themselves on launch.** If EchoForge has no usable engine
  but a model is already on your Mac, it selects it for you - preferring one that suits the
  language you dictate, so a Chinese setup lands on a Chinese engine rather than an English
  one. Nothing is changed if your current engine works.
- **Nothing is chosen behind your back.** Picking an engine in Settings before downloading
  it stays picked, even if you record in the meantime.

## Failures now say what to do about them

When EchoForge cannot transcribe because no engine is set up, it now tells you instead of
discarding the recording:

- The main window shows **"No transcription engine is set up"** with a button that opens
  Settings.
- The recording indicator says **"No engine set up"** rather than disappearing as if the
  dictation had worked.
- **Your audio is kept.** The recording stays in your history marked as failed, carrying
  the reason. Once you have chosen an engine, the regenerate button on that recording
  transcribes it - so a dictation you made before noticing the problem is not lost.

## Everything else is unchanged

The same engines, models, first-run flow, permissions and shortcuts as 0.2.1. The Chinese
engines still write Simplified Chinese (簡體字) and EchoForge still does not convert it to
Traditional (繁體字).

## Requirements

- Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
- macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later

## Installing this build

This release is **not signed with an Apple Developer ID and not notarized**, so macOS
will refuse to open it on the first try - typically "the app is damaged and can't be
opened", or "Apple could not verify EchoForge is free of malware". Nothing is wrong with
the download; macOS simply cannot check who built it.

1. Open `EchoForge.dmg` and drag `EchoForge.app` into `Applications`.
   Do not run it from the mounted disk image.
2. Then do **one** of the following, once:
   - Right-click (or Control-click) the app in `Applications`, choose **Open**, and click
     **Open** in the dialog; or
   - run `xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/EchoForge.app` in Terminal
     (adjust the path if you installed it elsewhere).

Upgrading from 0.2.1 or 0.2.0 is a straight replace over the old app. Your recordings,
personal terms and downloaded models are kept. Because these builds are unsigned, macOS
treats each one as a new app for permission purposes, so it will ask for Microphone and
Accessibility again - and if a stale **EchoForge** entry is already listed under
**Privacy & Security → Accessibility**, remove it with the minus button and add the new
app back.

Full instructions, including how to use the Chinese engines and the basics of recording:
[docs/install.md](https://github.com/hsuanchenlin/OpenSuperWhisper/blob/master/docs/install.md).
