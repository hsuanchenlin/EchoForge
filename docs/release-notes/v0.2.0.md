# OpenSuperWhisper 0.2.0

Chinese dictation, on your Mac, without sending audio anywhere.

## Chinese transcription engines

Two Chinese speech engines are now offered alongside Whisper and Parakeet, both running
locally on the Neural Engine. Neither is bundled - you pick one and the app downloads it.

- **SenseVoice-Small (240 MB)** is the recommended default for Chinese. It punctuates,
  and it also handles Cantonese, English, Japanese and Korean. It writes spoken numbers
  as digits: say 三點二十分 and you get 3点20分.
- **Paraformer-large (zh) (653 MB)** is the accuracy alternative on Mandarin characters.
  It is Mandarin only and produces no punctuation at all.

Both write **Simplified Chinese (簡體字)**; the app does not convert the output to
Traditional (繁體字).

Where you meet them:

- **First run** - choose Chinese in the language menu and both engines appear at the top
  of the model list, SenseVoice-Small badged "Recommended for Chinese". If you dictate
  another language, the rows stay hidden rather than offering you ~900 MB of Mandarin
  models.
- **Settings → Model → Speech Recognition Engine** - the same two rows, with the same
  caveats, download sizes and model attribution links. Selecting a Mandarin-only engine
  moves the transcription language with it, so the picker never promises something the
  engine cannot do.

After downloading, an engine spends about a minute preparing for the Neural Engine before
your first dictation. That happens once, and the app has not hung while it does.

## Requirements

- Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
- macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later

## Installing this build

This release is **not signed with an Apple Developer ID and not notarized**, so macOS
will refuse to open it on the first try - typically "the app is damaged and can't be
opened", or "Apple could not verify OpenSuperWhisper is free of malware". Nothing is
wrong with the download; macOS simply cannot check who built it.

1. Open `OpenSuperWhisper.dmg` and drag `OpenSuperWhisper.app` into `Applications`.
   Do not run it from the mounted disk image.
2. Then do **one** of the following, once:
   - Right-click (or Control-click) the app in `Applications`, choose **Open**, and click
     **Open** in the dialog; or
   - run `xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OpenSuperWhisper.app` in Terminal
     (adjust the path if you installed it elsewhere).

macOS will then ask for Microphone and Accessibility permission, plus Input Monitoring if
you bind recording to a single modifier key such as Right ⌥.

Full instructions, including how to use the Chinese engines and the basics of recording:
[docs/install.md](https://github.com/hsuanchenlin/OpenSuperWhisper/blob/master/docs/install.md).
